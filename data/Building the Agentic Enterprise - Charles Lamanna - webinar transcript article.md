# Building the Agentic Enterprise | Charles Lamanna, Microsoft

YouTube recording: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3dsqpcj-G_E

Speaker: Charles Lamanna, EVP of Agents and Business Applications, Microsoft

## Key Points

- Lamanna argues that enterprise AI has already moved beyond chat into action-oriented agents that manage calendars, synthesize customer context, generate apps, and execute workflows.
- His recommended adoption pattern is a two-sided move: give strong AI tools broadly across the workforce, then pick a small number of top-down business-critical AI projects with executive ownership.
- He warns against vanity metrics such as agent counts and says ROI should be measured against existing business outcomes like revenue growth, customer satisfaction, retention, cost to serve, and support load.
- For autonomy, he recommends building evals for the current non-AI process first, then inserting AI with human review, measuring approval rates, and only expanding automation when the data shows the AI is outperforming the previous process.
- On security, he frames an AI agent as the most gullible employee in the company and says standard controls still apply: least privilege, just-in-time access, and approvals for sensitive actions.
- In Microsoft engineering teams, he says some products now ship changes through coding-agent workflows with no direct human code writing, and he cites customer-facing improvements such as a 40% drop in app load time and a 25% drop in support tickets on one mature product.
- He expects team design to shift toward smaller, more senior, architecture-heavy groups with explicit token budgets, and argues that by the end of 2026 agentic work patterns will reach every business function.
